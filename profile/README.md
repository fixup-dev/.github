**fix-up** is a marketing & business-promotion agency based in Israel. We help companies grow with paid acquisition, organic search, CRM and operations automation, eCommerce, and brand design — building the systems that turn marketing spend into measurable results.

## What we do

- **Marketing & Promotion** — Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO, content writing, social planning & management.
- **CRM, Operations & Automation** — work, task & project management, CRM, lead routing, calendar, finance, brand and team operations.
- **eCommerce** — Shopify and WooCommerce builds, conversion optimization, abandoned-cart recovery, payment integrations, inventory.
- **Design & Branding** — brand identity, video editing, social creatives, web banners.
